Complexity comparison: app/main.py refactor
=============================================

Before:
- Highest complexity: is_policy_violation - B (7)
- Average complexity: A (2.75) across 8 blocks

After:
- Highest complexity: simple_summarize - B (6) (unchanged; not part of this refactor)
- Average complexity: A (2.0) across 11 blocks
- detect_policy_violation (formerly is_policy_violation): B (7) -> A (4)
- summarize: A (4) -> A (2)

Outcome:
- Tests remained green (10/10 passing before and after).
- Endpoint responsibilities were separated: policy detection
  (detect_policy_violation), source_id resolution (resolve_source_id),
  and response construction (build_refusal_response /
  build_summary_response) are now standalone, docstringed helpers
  instead of being inlined in the summarize() endpoint.
- Policy keyword lists were consolidated into POLICY_KEYWORDS so the
  detection logic is a single loop instead of repeated if/return blocks.
- Safety behavior remained unchanged: same keywords, same policy codes,
  same refusal message, verified by the full pytest suite.
